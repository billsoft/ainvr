## Reproduced cause

BPU inference averaged 38.33 ms (p95 46.43 ms), but synchronous review frame admission averaged 562.70 ms (p95 3010.85 ms). Source 29.48 FPS and delivered 14.81 FPS exceeded the 10 FPS target. Recognition archive admission was not blocking.

The durable alarm consumer checkpoint was stuck at offset 2182593. Replaying its pending item with the real repository/context reproduced `EvidenceImages.put: evidence_capacity`. Mongo context evidence reached the configured 1 GiB quota with 30-day retention. The producer SSD spool then reached its 2 GiB ceiling: about 8,776 unacknowledged JPEGs and 14,825 pin markers. The bounded producer queue applied backpressure, leaving BPU idle. Merely updating tracking policy did not remove this blockage.

## Operational recovery

- Published alarm configuration version 12 through the existing PublishOrchestrator, advisory lock, configuration validation and service pull/apply flow.
- Review context quota: 1 GiB → 8 GiB. Retention remains 30 days. Approximately 105 GiB SSD free after deployment.
- Briefly paused AI and LLM for controlled backlog recovery, then restored both. Original input stream and recorder services were retained. No evidence or historical alarms were deleted.
- The pinned context pool must not evict images referenced by unexpired alarms. This is a deliberate retention guarantee; it is not a license to drop evidence when full.

## Deployment checks and limits

- Size retained alarm evidence from actual image volume and retention period; the 1 GiB default was insufficient for this board. 8 GiB is a bounded board setting, not a guarantee that any event volume fits 30 days.
- `/readyz` verifies the store but does not by itself prove consumer progress. Check authenticated `/v1/status` observation_journal pending/failed/last_persisted_at, evidence capacity and AI review_frame_offer timing.
- Historical backlog and old capture faults remain visible and replayed; an absent historical source image cannot be recreated by changing the quota.
- If the 8 GiB retained pool fills before expiry, admission will again stop. Watch capacity and consumer progress before the limit; do not claim this operation removes the need for capacity monitoring.
- One channel at approximately 10 FPS legitimately uses around 40% BPU because each detection takes around 38–46 ms. A 90% target is not required for a 10 FPS channel.
- This is a short single-channel deployment verification, not multi-channel or long-duration certification.

## Scope of the capacity recovery

The quota increase and deployment above restore throughput. Nonblocking isolation of evidence/journal admission and bounded terminal handling of a full retention pool remain a proposed follow-up, not implemented by this release. The 8 GiB setting is private configuration on .190, not a changed source default or an applied .189 setting.
